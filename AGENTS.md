# scbase Content Work

For Supercell article drafting, editing, Obsidian/Hugo intake, or monthly Clash Royale balance coverage, read [skills/scbase-editor/SKILL.md](skills/scbase-editor/SKILL.md) and only the references relevant to the request.

Article sources live in `content/posts/`; `content/` is the active Obsidian vault. The workflow ends at local intake: the user edits the article again in Obsidian and manually runs `pub` to synchronize to WeChat. This skill does not run the publisher or manage remote drafts, uploads, pushes, or public release.

For unrelated code, SEO, or card-database work, follow the relevant repository files instead of applying the article workflow.
